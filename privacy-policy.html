Official Policy

Privacy Policy for Sela Holy Bible

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 • Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Sacred Trust Summary: Sela Holy Bible is built with reverence for your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not host third-party advertisements, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Your prayers, notes, and spiritual reflections are private.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sela Holy Bible ("Sela", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information necessary to deliver a personalized, sacred spiritual experience:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and optional display name. This is used solely to authenticate your identity and synchronize your reading data across devices.
  • Spiritual Reflections & Journaling: Any private prayer notes, journal entries, bookmarks, and verse highlights you create are stored in encrypted databases associated strictly with your authenticated account.
  • Spiritual Stage & Goals: Responses provided during the 5-question onboarding flow (spiritual stage, devotional time preferences, virtues) are stored to tailor daily reading plans and reminders to your spiritual walk.
  • Notification Preferences: Custom reminder schedules and alert times (e.g., Morning Devotional or Evening Prayer) configured within the app.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect financial information, credit card numbers, or billing details (Sela is free to use).
  • We do NOT track your precise GPS location.
  • We do NOT collect Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA) or engage in cross-app advertising tracking.
  • We do NOT access your contacts, microphone, camera, or SMS messages.

4. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for:

  • Authenticating your account and keeping your session secure.
  • Synchronizing your Bible reading progress, daily streaks, bookmarks, and notes across sessions.
  • Delivering scheduled local notifications for daily scripture reflections and prayer prompts.
  • Providing technical customer support when you reach out to us.

5. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

To provide reliable, high-performance cloud synchronization, we partner with trusted cloud infrastructure providers that adhere to rigorous security standards:

  • Supabase: For secure user authentication and encrypted PostgreSQL database hosting.
  • Firebase / Google Cloud: For real-time synchronization of public community prayer requests and cloud infrastructure.

All data transmitted between the app and our cloud services is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).

6. Advertising & Data Selling

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data, devotional reflections, or reading habits to advertising brokers, data aggregators, or third parties. The app contains zero commercial third-party advertising networks.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your data at all times.

How to Delete Your Account: You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data (including notes, highlights, and profile information) at any time by emailing us at selabibleapp@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We process all deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Sela Holy Bible is intended for general spiritual enrichment. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we take immediate steps to remove that information from our servers.

9. Permissions Used by the App

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to deliver optional daily verse reflections and prayer reminders at the time you choose.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to reliably reschedule your devotional reminders if your device is restarted.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to access Scripture translations, cloud sync, and community prayer updates.
